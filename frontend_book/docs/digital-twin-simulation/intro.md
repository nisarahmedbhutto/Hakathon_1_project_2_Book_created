# Introduction to The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)

Welcome to the module on Digital Twins for simulation. This module is designed for AI engineers and robotics students who understand ROS 2 fundamentals and want to simulate humanoid robots in realistic physical environments.

## Overview

This module explores how digital twins enable the simulation of humanoid robots, physical laws, environments, and sensors before real-world deployment. You'll learn about:

- What digital twins are in robotics
- Why simulation is critical for humanoid robots
- Introduction to Gazebo as a physics simulator
- Physics concepts: gravity, collisions, friction, and joints

## Structure

The module is organized into three chapters:

1. **Digital Twins and Physics-Based Simulation**: Understanding digital twins and physics simulation
2. **Environment Building with Gazebo and Unity**: Creating simulated worlds and loading robots
3. **Simulating Robot Sensors**: Understanding simulated sensors for Physical AI

## Learning Objectives

After completing this module, you will be able to:
- Explain how digital twins reduce real-world risk
- Understand basic physics simulation concepts
- Conceptually design simulation environments
- Differentiate between sensor types and their use cases

Let's begin with understanding digital twins and physics-based simulation.
