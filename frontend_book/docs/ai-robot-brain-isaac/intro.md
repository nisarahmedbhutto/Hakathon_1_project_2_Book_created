# Introduction to The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac™)

Welcome to the module on AI-Robot Brain using NVIDIA Isaac. This module is designed for AI engineers and robotics students who are familiar with ROS 2 and simulation environments and want to build advanced perception and navigation systems for humanoid robots.

## Overview

This module explores how NVIDIA Isaac can be used to train, perceive, and navigate humanoid robots through photorealistic simulation and hardware-accelerated robotics pipelines. You'll learn about:

- The NVIDIA Isaac ecosystem and its role in Physical AI systems
- Photorealistic simulation and synthetic data generation for perception models
- Hardware-accelerated perception and localization concepts
- Navigation and path planning for bipedal humanoid robots

## Structure

The module is organized into three chapters:

1. **Chapter 1**: NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Synthetic Data - Overview of the Isaac ecosystem and synthetic data generation
2. **Chapter 2**: Isaac ROS and Hardware-Accelerated Perception - Architecture and perception concepts
3. **Chapter 3**: Navigation and Path Planning with Nav2 - Navigation challenges and path planning concepts

## Learning Objectives

After completing this module, you will be able to:
- Explain the role of NVIDIA Isaac in Physical AI systems
- Understand why synthetic data is critical for robotics
- Describe the benefits of photorealistic simulation
- Understand perception pipelines in humanoid robots
- Explain VSLAM at a system level
